# TextSnap Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 September 2026

TextSnap is an app that extracts text from images, scans QR codes and translates text on your device.

## No account

TextSnap has no sign-in, no registration and no user account. We do not ask for your name, email address or any other identifier, and we do not create a profile for you.

Earlier versions of TextSnap used Google Sign-In. That has been removed — the app now opens straight to the scan screen and no account data is collected or stored.

## Images and extracted text

When you take a photo or choose an image, TextSnap reads the text on your device using Google ML Kit. Your images and the text extracted from them are **not uploaded to our servers**.

Recognised text, a reference to the source image and a small preview are saved in a local history on your device so you can reopen earlier scans. The last 30 scans are kept. You can delete a single scan or clear the whole history from inside the app, and everything is removed when you uninstall.

## Translation

Translation also runs on your device. The first time you use a language, Google ML Kit downloads that language pack from Google's servers; this needs an internet connection and is subject to Google's own privacy policy. After the download, translation works offline. The text you translate is not sent anywhere.

To tell a missing language pack apart from a failed download, the app makes a connectivity check to a Google endpoint (`clients3.google.com/generate_204`). That request contains no personal data.

## QR codes

QR and barcode scanning happens in the camera preview on your device. Scanned codes are stored in a local history of the last 40 codes, which you can clear in the app. Opening a scanned link hands it to your browser.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — to take photos to scan and to read QR codes. Nothing is recorded or stored beyond the photo you choose to scan.
- **Photos / media access** — only so you can pick an image to scan, through your device's own picker.

## Sharing and export

When you copy, share or save text as a TXT or PDF file, you choose the destination through your device's share sheet. TextSnap does not send that content anywhere on its own.

## Ads

TextSnap shows ads supplied by **Google AdMob**. To do that the Google Mobile Ads
SDK is included in the app, and it may access your device's **advertising ID** —
a resettable identifier Android provides for advertising. You can reset it, or
switch it off entirely, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

TextSnap requests **non-personalised ads**, so the ads you see are not based on a
profile of your interests. Google may still process the advertising ID together
with technical information such as device type and approximate region in order to
deliver and measure ads; that processing is covered by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region
where consent is required, TextSnap asks for your choices before any ad is
requested, using Google's consent form. You can change those choices at any time
from **Settings → Ad privacy options** inside the app.

Ads never see the text you extract, the images you scan, or your scan history —
those stay on your device and are not shared with Google or anyone else.

## Third-party services

- **Google ML Kit** — on-device text recognition, language identification and translation.
- **Google AdMob** — ad serving, as described above.

TextSnap contains no analytics SDK and no tracking beyond the ad serving described
above. It does not collect usage statistics.

## Children

TextSnap is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them. Apart from the advertising ID used to serve ads, the app collects no personal data.

## Your data

Everything TextSnap stores stays on your device:

- Scan history and QR history — clear them in the app, or uninstall.
- Language and theme preference — removed when you uninstall.

There is no account to delete and no server-side copy of your scans. The advertising
ID is held by your device, not by us — reset or disable it in Android Settings.
