# TextSnap Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 8, 2026

TextSnap is an image-to-text OCR application.

## Information We Collect

TextSnap uses Google Sign-In through Firebase Authentication. When you sign in, Firebase may provide basic account information such as your name, email address, and profile identifier so the app can maintain your account session.

TextSnap lets you select images from your device or take photos with your camera. These images are used for text recognition on your device.

## How We Use Information

We use your Google account only to sign you in.

Selected images are used only to extract text. Text recognition is performed on your device. TextSnap does not upload your selected images to our own servers for OCR processing.

Recognized text may be saved in local history on your device so you can reopen previous scans. You can delete history from inside the app.

## Sharing and Export

When you choose to copy, share, or export recognized text as TXT or PDF, the destination is selected by you through your device's share sheet.

## Account Deletion

TextSnap includes an in-app account deletion option. Deleting your account signs you out, requests deletion of your Firebase Authentication account, and clears local scan history on the device.

## Third-Party Services

TextSnap uses:

- Firebase Authentication for Google Sign-In
- Google ML Kit Text Recognition for on-device OCR
- Google Mobile Ads for advertising
