# TextSnap Account Deletion

TextSnap allows users to delete their account from inside the app.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open TextSnap.
2. Sign in with your Google account.
3. Open the account/settings area.
4. Select Delete Account.
5. Confirm the deletion.

Deleting your account requests deletion of your Firebase Authentication account and clears local scan history stored on your device.

## Request account deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by contacting:

norbytelabs@gmail.com

Please contact us from the same email address used for Google Sign-In so we can verify the request.

## Data deleted

- Firebase Authentication account
- Local scan history stored by the app on your device, when deletion is performed in the app

TextSnap does not upload selected images to our own servers for OCR processing.
