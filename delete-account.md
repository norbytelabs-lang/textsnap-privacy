TextSnap — Account and Data Deletion
Effective date: 5 September 2026
TextSnap has no user accounts.
Earlier versions of the app used Google Sign-In. That was removed: TextSnap now opens straight to the scan screen, and there is nothing to register for, sign in to, or delete on our side.
What TextSnap stores
Everything the app keeps is stored locally on your own device:
Your recent scans (the recognised text, a reference to the source image, and a small preview) — the last 30 are kept
Your recent QR scans — the last 40 are kept
Your language and theme preference
None of this is sent to us or to any server.
How to delete it
Inside the app
Open Settings → Clear history to delete every saved scan, or
Open History and delete individual scans, or
Open the QR tab and clear the scanned-code list
By uninstalling
Uninstalling TextSnap removes all of the above from your device.
