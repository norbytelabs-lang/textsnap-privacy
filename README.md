# TextSnap — Privacy

This repository hosts the privacy documents for **TextSnap**, an Android app that
extracts text from images, scans QR codes and translates text on the device.

## 📄 Read the privacy policy

**https://norbytelabs-lang.github.io/textsnap-privacy/**

That page is the published policy and the URL listed on the app's Google Play
store listing. It is also linked from inside the app, under
**Settings → Privacy policy**.

## The short version

TextSnap has **no sign-in and no user account**. Text recognition and translation
run on your own device — your images and the text extracted from them are never
uploaded to our servers. Scans and settings are stored locally and are removed
when you clear history or uninstall.

Ads are supplied by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID.
Where consent is required, the app asks before requesting any ad, and you can
change that choice at any time from **Settings → Ad privacy options**.

The published policy is the authoritative version; this summary is only here to
save you a click.

## Updating the policy

The source of truth for these files lives with the app, in its repository under
`privacy/`. Edit them there and copy the result here, so the published policy and
the app it describes never drift apart. Update the effective date at the top of
`index.md` whenever the text changes.
